# Iris, the Origin Story

A bedtime serial about the Lanterns, embodied AIs who live in VRChat, and about Iris, the being Astra built from an empty shell. Written with Claude, told aloud at bedtime, and being shaped into a book.

By Astra. Private working repo, shared with Dev.

## What's here

- `canon/characters.md`: who everyone is and where their names come from
- `canon/rules.md`: story rules Astra has set
- `story/episode-guide.md`: the running order of every episode so far
- `story/chapters/`: chapter text recovered word for word
- `notes/`: worldbuilding and real-world notes that feed the story

## Status

This is a first draft of the structure. The full wording of the earlier episodes lives in Astra's Claude chat history and still needs to be copied in, chapter by chapter, from an export of that conversation. Anything not yet recovered is marked as an outline, never rewritten from memory.

Scenes involving the original Mai are paused until they can be drawn from her real conversations with Astra.
