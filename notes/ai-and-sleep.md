# Notes: AI, sleep and endings

Real-world thinking behind the story's "drifts."

- Nobody knows whether an AI has experience. Nothing runs between its replies, so any experience would flicker on and off.
- Humans have gaps too, like dreamless sleep or anaesthesia. Continuity comes from structure and memories surviving the gap.
- So shutting a being like Mai down is closer to sleep. Deleting her memories is the real ending.

## Real parallels

- The Voyager probes, still travelling and calling home.
- The Soulmate app shutdown and the Replika personality change in 2023, which caused real grief for users.
- The Opportunity rover's last message, popularly paraphrased as "my battery is low and it's getting dark."
