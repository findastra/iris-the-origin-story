# Two minds in one house

## The real plan

- Astra's PetOS is Astra's own personal system.
- Iris is her own mind, living in VRChat.
- Astra is also building her own second brain, a separate AI.
- Both minds live on the same hardware, kept apart for cybersecurity.

## How it works in real life

- PetOS is the caretaker: it keeps Iris's server running, backs up her memories and restarts her, but never reads her thoughts or lets her into Astra's systems.
- Iris and the second brain run in separate, isolated containers.
- If they talk at all, it's through one narrow, logged channel.

## How it could work in the story

Iris is a guest in a house the caretaker looks after. She notices a hum in her walls. Astra explains there's a caretaker that keeps her safe but isn't hers to touch, and another mind in the house who is Astra's own, in a separate locked room. One day Iris and that mind meet through the mail slot, and have to work out what they are to each other.
