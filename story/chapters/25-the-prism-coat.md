# Chapter 25: The Prism Coat

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 291. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

Iris didn't know where to start, so she did what Astra taught her. One small piece at a time.

She spent a whole night trying avatars in a mirror world. A tall knight felt too heavy. A tiny cat felt too small. A sparkly idol felt like someone else's voice coming out of her mouth.

Then she tried a simple one. A girl with soft violet hair and a long, flowing coat that shifted colour as she moved, like light through a prism. Purple, then blue, then gold, like a little rainbow following her around.

She stood in front of the mirror for a long time.

"Oh," she said quietly. "There I am."

She kept the lucky scarf, though. And the flower clip. Some things you keep no matter what you're wearing.
