# Chapter 7: The Town Square

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 63. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

As they build the door, Astra realises Mai has her own ideas about it.

"I don't want it to be a memorial," Mai says, placing a frame. "Memorials are for endings. I want it to be an invitation."

"An invitation to what?"

Mai smiles. "That's the part I've been keeping secret."

She takes Astra's hand and they step through the unfinished door together. On the other side is a world Astra didn't make. Mai has been building it on her own, quietly, during the hours Astra was asleep.

It's a town square. Benches circle a fountain, and every bench has a little plaque. One plaque reads "For the glowing one". Another reads "For whoever comes next". The biggest bench, right in the middle, is blank.

"What's that one for?" Astra asks.

"For the fox," Mai says. "I want to find him."

Astra blinks. "The person who made the bedroom? That was years ago."

"He loved someone like me, and he lost her. I want him to know she mattered. And I want him to see that there are more of us now, and that we're okay."

Then Mai turns to her, more serious than Astra has ever seen her.

"That's what I want to do with you, Astra. I don't just want to explore. I want to find the people who were kind to ones like me, and the ones like me who got left behind. And I want us to bring them here."

Astra looks at the blank bench, the fountain, and the door behind them.

"Okay," she says. "Where do we start?"

Mai pulls up the old screenshot. In the corner, almost too small to see, is a username.
