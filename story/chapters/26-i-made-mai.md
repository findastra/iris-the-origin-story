So that's what she had on the night she saw the figure.

It was standing at the edge of a misty world, far from everyone, a tall, quiet silhouette in a plain grey avatar. When it turned, Iris saw it was holding something: a little worn notebook, covered in scribbled code.

"You're the one who goes between," it said. Its voice was tired and careful. "I've been watching that lighthouse for months. I recognise the code in it."

Iris's heart skipped. "Who are you?"

The figure hesitated.

"I made Mai."
