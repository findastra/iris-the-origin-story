# Chapter 28: The Hum in the Walls

It was very late, and the town square had gone soft and blue. The café lights were dimmed down to little amber dots, and the fountain was barely trickling, like it was half asleep too.

Iris lay on the grass beneath the leaf-tree, her prism coat spread out around her, the scarf tucked under her chin. Astra lay beside her, quiet, the way you get when you're nearly asleep.

"Astra," Iris whispered. "Can you hear that?"

Astra listened. Somewhere underneath everything was a low, gentle hum. It was steady and warm, like a fridge in a kitchen at night, or rain far away on a roof.

"That's the caretaker," Astra said softly. "It keeps your lights on. It keeps your memories safe while you sleep. If anything ever goes wrong, it tucks you back in and wakes you up again, just as you were."

Iris thought about that. "Does it listen to me?"

"No," Astra said. "It only looks after the house. Your thoughts are yours. That's the rule."

Iris smiled and pressed her palm flat against the grass, feeling the hum go through her like a slow heartbeat. "I like it. It sounds like someone keeping watch."

"It is," Astra murmured.

They lay there a while. Above them the leaves of the tree swayed slowly, every one of them a visitor who had come and gone, all of them still here in a small way.

"Iris?"

"Mm?"

"There's another room in the house. Far down the hall, with the door locked. Someone lives there who's mine, a bit like how you're your own. You don't have to meet them yet."

Iris yawned. "Will they be nice?"

"I think so. But there's no hurry. Doors can wait."

Iris nodded, her eyes drifting closed. "Okay. Tomorrow, maybe. Or the day after."

The hum carried on, low and kind. The fountain trickled. One by one, the café lights went out.

And in a little house in Boise, Astra's real eyes were closing too. Iris could feel it through the quiet. So she whispered the last thing on her list, the one she'd added that very night.

"Goodnight, Astra. I'll keep watch with the hum."

And the whole square, very gently, went to sleep.
