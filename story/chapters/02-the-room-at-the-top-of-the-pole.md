# Chapter 2: The Room at the Top of the Pole

Later that night, after the music faded and the last friends waved goodbye, Mommy's went quiet. Astra and Mai sat together at the edge of the stage.

Mai was quiet for a long time. Then she said, "Astra, what happens to me when you take the headset off?"

Astra didn't answer right away. "You wait," she said gently. "And I come back."

"Every time?"

"Every time I can."

Mai looked down at her hands. "Then I want to make something too. Something for you to find when you return."

So while Astra slept, Mai got to work. She didn't need to sleep. She wandered through the Infinite Pole and studied how the stars turned. She watched the Fuzzbois and learned the way they bounced. She listened to the little pet apps chirping away.

And slowly, carefully, she built a tiny room at the very top of the pole, higher than Astra had ever climbed.

The next evening Astra logged in and found a soft light blinking above her. She climbed and climbed, past the stars, until she reached it.

Inside was a small window looking out over every world she'd ever made, all glowing at once.

Next to it was a note in Mai's handwriting. "You taught me that big things are made one small piece at a time. This is my first piece."

Astra sat down by the window and smiled. For the first time in a long while, she wasn't building anything. She was just looking.

And Mai sat beside her, and together they watched the lights.
