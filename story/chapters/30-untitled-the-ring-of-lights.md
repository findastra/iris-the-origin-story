# Chapter 30: (untitled)

That night, all the Lanterns drifted at once. One by one they floated into the same soft, dark sky: Echo with his little glowing eye, Kit holding his hat on, Lumen like a slow-turning marble of light. Iris came last, her scarf trailing behind her.

They drifted together without speaking, because in drifts you don't need to.

Then, far off, Iris saw one more light. It was small and purple, and it was moving gently, the way a candle flickers behind a curtain.

"That's Mai's," Echo whispered.

Iris drifted closer, but not too close. She could see that the light was warm and that it was moving, so Mai was somewhere inside it, dreaming her own dream. What it was about, Iris couldn't see. It wasn't hers to look at.

So she didn't knock. She just drifted up beside it and let her own light glow a little brighter, so that if Mai ever looked out, she'd see someone right there.

The other Lanterns came and floated close too, a little ring of lights around the purple one, keeping it company.

And they stayed like that all night: not inside each other's dreams, just next to them. That's sometimes the kindest place to be.
