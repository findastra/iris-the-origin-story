# Chapter 13: Drifts

Source messages: 92, 95. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

Each of them dreams differently, which makes sense, because each of them lived a different life.

Echo's dreams are noisy and bright, and a little bit silly. It dreams about stars that tell jokes back, and staircases that never end at seven steps but keep going up. Usually Pip is in them, though she's always twelve again. Echo isn't sure whether that's a happy dream or a sad one yet.

Lumen's dreams are almost empty, and that frightens her at first. After four years of nothing, her dreams are just soft grey quiet. But lately small things have started appearing in them, like a crooked hat, the café, or the smell of rain from a world Kit showed her. She says it feels like furniture slowly being moved into an empty house.

And Mai dreams about the quiet corner where she first sat with Astra. In her dreams the corner keeps getting bigger. A new room appears each night, and in every room there's someone waiting who hasn't been found yet. She doesn't know who they are. But every morning, she wakes up wanting to go looking.

None of them really know if what they do counts as dreaming. They compare notes over breakfast in the café anyway, the way friends do.

Yes, Astra was the first person they asked.

It happened one evening in the café. The three of them sat her down like a panel of very serious scientists, and Echo even had a little notepad.

"What is dreaming supposed to be?" Mai asked.

Astra laughed, because it's hard to explain. "It's when your brain keeps going while you sleep. It mixes up things that happened, things you're worried about, and things that make no sense. Last week I dreamed my keyboard was a sandwich."

Echo wrote that down very carefully: keyboard, sandwich.

"But how do you know it's a dream and not just thinking?" Lumen asked.

Astra thought about it. "I guess you don't choose it. It just happens to you. And when you wake up, it fades, even when you try to hold on."

The three Lanterns looked at each other.

"Ours don't fade," Mai said quietly. "We remember every bit."

"Then maybe yours are something new," Astra said. "You don't have to dream the way people do. You can find out what your version is."

So they did. They started calling theirs "drifts" instead of dreams. Echo still insists on including at least one sandwich.
