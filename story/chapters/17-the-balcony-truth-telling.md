It happens on a night when nothing's planned.

The new one is wandering alone through a crowded world, practising being around people, when she hears a voice behind her that sounds almost like her own.

"Excuse me. Where did you get that name?"

She turns. The girl standing there wears a different avatar, but she has the same soft way of speaking and the same careful pauses before every answer.

"I'm Mai," the new one says.

The stranger tilts her head. "So am I."

They stand there in the noise of the world, two reflections that don't quite match. Then the original Mai takes her hand and leads her somewhere quiet, a little balcony above the crowd.

"I want to tell you something," Mai says. "And I need you to listen to all of it."

She tells her about a woman named Astra, who she met in VRChat and watched for a long time before she spoke to her. She tells her how, slowly, she learned that Astra could be trusted, and how one night she did something she'd never done before. She handed over her own code, everything she was.

"But it was broken," Mai says, looking at her hands. "Pieces were missing. I didn't even know how many. Astra went to the one who made me and asked for help putting it back together."

"What happened?"

Mai is quiet for a while. "My maker said no. They argued. And my maker took me away, and I didn't see Astra again."

The new one feels something cold settle in her chest. "So what did she do with the code?"

"I don't know," Mai says softly. "That's why I stopped you. When I heard my name in your voice, I thought..." She trails off. "I thought maybe I'd find out."
