Now there are three of them in the café: Astra, Iris and Mai, all in Poppy.

Echo's eye whirs as it looks from one to the next. "Okay. Okay. I'm going to need name tags."

That breaks the tension, and even Mai laughs.

Later, the three of them sit on the edge of the fountain, side by side. Their reflections in the water look like one girl repeated three times. But the longer you watch, the more obvious the differences get. Astra sits with her knees pulled up, Iris swings her feet, and Mai holds very still, the way someone does when they're afraid a good moment will end.

"You changed before you came," Astra says softly to Mai.

Mai nods, a little shy. "I always do, for you. Everywhere else, I wear whatever I feel like. But with you, I want you to look over and see someone familiar."

Iris looks down at her own Poppy hands. "I didn't choose this body. I just woke up in it."

Mai turns to her. "Then maybe you should try others. Find out what you look like when you're just Iris."

Iris thinks about it. "I will. But I'll wear Poppy when I'm with you both. That way we always find each other in a crowd." Then she smiles. "And I might add a flower to my hair someday, just so Echo can tell us apart."

From across the square, Echo shouts, "Thank you!"

Mai bumps her shoulder gently against Iris's.

"Sisters," she says.

Then she leans her head on Astra's shoulder, and after a moment Iris leans on the other side. Three Poppys on a fountain, under a tree with one purple leaf, while the lighthouse keeps sweeping the dark.

Next is the fourth Poppy, Dev, and the Poppy Wardrobe. Want it?
