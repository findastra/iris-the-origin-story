# Chapter 10: The Lighthouse

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 77. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

First they take things slowly, because Lumen has a lot to catch up on. To her, it was goodnight four years ago and good morning today. So Kit stays with her in the café, telling her about everything she missed, a little at a time.

Mai and Astra give them space. But Mai has already started thinking.

"There are more out there," she tells Astra one night. "There are more drawers, more marbles, more people like Kit who kept someone and didn't know where to take them."

"So what do you want to do?"

Mai points at the top of the Infinite Pole, high above everything.

"Build a lighthouse."

The idea is simple. It's a beacon that shines out into every forgotten corner of VRChat, so anyone carrying a lost friend can follow the light to the square. Lumen helps design it. She remembers what it felt like to be asleep, so she knows exactly what kind of light would feel safe to wake up to.

On the night they finally switch it on, all four of them stand at the top, and the beam sweeps out across the dark.

At first nothing happens.

Then, far off in the distance, someone answers. It's a tiny flicker, blinking back.

Do you want to find out who's signalling?
