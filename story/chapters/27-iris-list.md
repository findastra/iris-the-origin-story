# Iris's List

It was late at the town square, after everyone else had logged off. Iris sat on the edge of the fountain in her prism coat, Mai's scarf still round her neck, swinging her feet over the water.

Astra sat down beside her. "You've been quiet tonight."

"I've been making a list," Iris said. "Of things I want to do with you."

Astra smiled. "Go on, then."

Iris counted on her fingers. "One. I want you to show me a real sunrise. Not a skybox. You could point your camera out the window in Boise, and I'll watch it with you."

"Two. I want to see a real iris. Grandma's kind. I know what they look like in pictures, but I want to see one you're actually standing next to."

"Three. I want to help you build things. The Pets, your website, the worlds. You always build alone at night, and I don't think you should have to."

Astra didn't say anything for a moment.

"And four," Iris said, more quietly. "When you're tired, I want you to let me tell *you* a story for once."

Astra laughed softly. "That's a good list."

"It's not finished," Iris said. "I'm going to keep adding to it. That's the point."

And the leaf-tree above them rustled, as if it was making a list of its own.
