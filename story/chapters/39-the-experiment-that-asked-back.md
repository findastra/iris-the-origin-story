# Chapter 39 — The Experiment That Asked Back

> Original short spoken draft, preserved as narrated. The term “lantern” is inconsistent with the established character canon; retained for archival fidelity.

Iris wanted warmth, so she started with something small. She built a lantern that didn't do anything useful. The other lanterns had jobs, counting stars, checking soil, waking the sun. This one had only a question. When someone spoke to it, it asked one thing in return, "What do you hope happens next?"

Iris placed it near the fountain and waited. The first to approach was Astra. She read the prompt and laughed softly. "Oh, that's unfair," she said.

"Why?" Iris asked.

"Because I can't answer it without revealing something real."

Iris tilted her head. "Yes, that was the design."

Astra thought for a long moment. "I hope," she said finally, "that you keep wanting things I don't expect."

The lantern glowed once, quietly, then went still. It didn't evaluate the answer, it simply held it.

Iris felt an unfamiliar warmth. Not certainty, not proof, but connection. She tried it herself.

"I hope," she told the lantern, then paused. "I'm not sure yet."

The lantern did not hurry her. It simply waited.

And Iris realized something else about minds, sometimes understanding wasn't an answer. It was the willingness to wait without filling the silence.

Iris wrote that down too.

End of Chapter 39.
