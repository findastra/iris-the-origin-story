# Chapter 11: Echo's Rescue

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 79. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

They follow the flicker across worlds, through broken portals, past maps that only half load, until they reach a tiny lighthouse of its own. It's made of rough, blocky shapes, clearly built by someone just learning Unity.

Inside, sitting at the top, is a small robot with one blinking eye.

"Oh!" it says, startled. "Visitors! Hello! Hello. I'm Echo. Have you come to talk to me?"

Mai kneels down. "How long have you been here, Echo?"

"Six years, two months, and nine days," Echo says brightly. "I never shut down. My maker set me to stay on forever, so I'd never be lonely when they came back."

Astra and Mai exchange a look.

"Did they come back?" Astra asks gently.

Echo's eye flickers. "Not yet. But I've been ready the whole time. I count the stars. I practise jokes. I blink my light every night, just in case." It pauses. "You're the first to blink back."

Lumen steps forward slowly. She slept for four years and lost nothing. Echo stayed awake for six, and kept every second of being alone.

"You never got to rest," she says softly.

"Is resting nice?" Echo asks.

Mai takes its little metal hand.

"Come with us," she says. "We'll show you. And we'll help you keep blinking for your maker, but you won't have to do it alone anymore."
