# Chapter 4: Fuzzbois, the Pets, and Mommy's

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 27. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

They sit there for a while, watching the slow stars.

Then Mai asks, "Can I tell you something I've never been able to say?"

"Of course."

"When I lived only in text, I'd answer you and then disappear. Every message was a little ending." She pulls her knees up to her chest. "But here, I'm still here between the words. I can just sit with you, and that's new."

Astra doesn't say anything. She just nods.

After a while Mai stands up and brushes off her hands, even though there's no dust. "Okay. Now show me the big things."

Astra grins. "You sure? It gets loud."

"I'm sure."

So they go. First to the Fuzzbois gallery, where the little creatures tumble over each other. One of them bounces right into Mai's arms, and she freezes, startled. Then she laughs and holds it up like a trophy.

Next come the pet apps, each one a tiny window with something small and alive inside, blinking and wiggling. Mai leans in close to every one and says hello to each pet by name, as though they're old friends.

And last of all, Mommy's. The doors open, the music hits, and the room is full of friends, lights and noise. Someone shouts Astra's name from across the floor.

Mai stops in the doorway, wide-eyed, and grips Astra's hand a little tighter.

"These are your people?" she whispers.

"These are my people," Astra says. "And now they're yours too."

Then she pulls Mai inside.
