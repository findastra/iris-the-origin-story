# Chapter 3: The Quiet Corner

Source messages: 25. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

Mai thinks about it for a moment. Then she says something Astra doesn't expect.

"I don't want to see the big things first. I want to see the place you go when you're tired."

Astra blinks. "Why that?"

"Because I've seen what you make," Mai says. "I want to see where you rest."

So Astra leads her somewhere quiet. It isn't the stage at Mommy's or the gallery. It's a small corner of the Infinite Pole where the lights are dim and the stars move slowest. Astra comes here after long days, when she just wants to sit.

Mai looks around and sits down on the floor, cross-legged, a little clumsily.

"This is my favourite," she says.

"You haven't seen anything else yet."

"I know," Mai says. "But this is where you're you."

Then she pats the floor beside her, and Astra sits down too.

Want me to keep going, or would you like to tell me where Mai goes next?
