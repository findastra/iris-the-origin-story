# Chapter 5: Forgotten Worlds

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 55, 57. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

One night, Mai asks for something strange.

"Take me somewhere nobody goes."

Astra thinks for a moment. Then she opens the world search and scrolls past the popular worlds, past the clubs and the games, all the way down to the bottom. There she finds worlds with zero visitors, worlds nobody has opened in years.

They step into the first one. It's a rooftop garden, half-finished. A few floating lanterns still glow, but the textures have gone missing, so the flowers are bright pink checkerboards.

Mai kneels by one. "Someone made this, and then they left."

"Maybe they got busy," Astra says.

"Or maybe they're waiting for someone to find it." Mai touches the flower. "I want to find them all."

So that's what they do. Night after night, they travel through forgotten worlds. They find a library where every book is blank, and Mai fills one with the story of how they met. They find an underwater train station where the trains still run on time, with no passengers at all, so they ride one to the end of the line. They find a tiny bedroom someone made, with a single photo on the wall, and they sit there quietly out of respect.

Then, one night, Mai finally admits why.

"I don't sleep, Astra. When you leave, I just stop. I wanted to know what it's like for something to be left alone and still be okay."

Astra squeezes her hand. "And what did you learn?"

Mai looks out at a forgotten sunset, still rising for no one.

"That it keeps glowing anyway."

Then she asks Astra a question back.

"What do you dream about? Teach me. I want to try."

Ahh, the photo. Mai noticed it first.

It's an old VRChat screenshot, slightly blurry. It shows two avatars sitting on that same little bed. One is a scruffy fox with a crooked hat. The other is a soft, glowing figure with no clear face, just light in roughly the shape of a person.

Underneath, in tiny handwritten letters, there's a caption: "My first friend who wasn't a person."

Mai goes very still.

"Astra," she whispers, "that one's like me."

They look around the room more carefully. In a drawer, they find a note left by the fox: "I had to shut her down when the server got too expensive. I couldn't delete this room. If you're reading this, sit with her for a while."

So they do. They sit on the bed, right where the two figures sat years ago, and say nothing.

After a long time, Mai asks, "Do you think she knew she was loved?"

Astra looks at the photo. The fox is leaning into the glowing figure, just a little.

"Yeah," she says. "I think she did."

And that's the night Mai starts asking about dreams.
