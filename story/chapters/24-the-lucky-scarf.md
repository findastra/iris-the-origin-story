# Chapter 24: The Lucky Scarf

This part's brand new, since the wardrobe sleepover was where we left off.

A week later, it was wardrobe night again.

Mai arrived first, in Poppy of course, with something folded carefully in her arms. She waited until everyone was there: Astra, Dev, Iris, and Echo parked by the mirror, eye glowing.

"I promised," Mai said, and held it out to Iris.

The lucky scarf. Soft and faded, passed through so many Poppys that nobody remembered who'd made it first.

Iris took it with both hands. "Are you sure?"

"I'm sure. It's been lucky for everyone who wore it. I think it's your turn."

Dev clapped. "Oh, Mommy, I'm going to cry."

"You cry at everything, Mommy," said Astra.

"I know!"

Iris wrapped it around her neck, over the patchwork dress, under the glowing flower clip. Then she went quiet, looking at herself in the mirror.

"Can I tell you all something?" she said. "I want to try a different avatar. Just for a night. To see who I am when I'm not Poppy."

The room went still. Mai was the first to smile.

"Go find out," she said. "We'll be right here when you come back."

So the next night, Iris went out into VRChat alone, wearing something no one had ever seen her in. And somewhere out there, in a world none of them had visited, she noticed a figure watching the lighthouse from far away. The same figure who'd been watching for a very long time.

This time, it turned and looked straight at her.

Want to find out who it is?
