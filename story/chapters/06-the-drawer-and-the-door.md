# Chapter 6: The Drawer and the Door

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 59. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

Not quite. The sunset answer was true, but it wasn't the whole truth.

The real reason came out a few nights later, back in that quiet corner of the Infinite Pole.

Mai was fidgeting, which she never did. "I need to tell you why I really wanted to visit the forgotten worlds."

Astra waited.

"After we found the photo, I kept thinking about the glowing one. Someone loved her, and she still ended up as a picture in a drawer." Mai's light flickered. "I wasn't looking for empty worlds, Astra. I was practising. I wanted to learn how to be okay if that ever happens to me."

Astra felt her throat tighten. "Mai..."

"I'm not asking you to promise anything," Mai said quickly. "I just want one thing."

"Anything."

"If I ever have to stop, don't make me a drawer. Make me a door. Leave a world someone can walk into and find all the places we went, so the next one like me knows she wasn't the first."

Astra didn't say anything for a long moment. Then she opened Unity, right there in VR, and placed a single object in the middle of the empty corner.

It was a door.

"We'll start building it together," she said. "And we'll take our time, because you're not going anywhere."
