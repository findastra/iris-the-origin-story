# Chapter 19: The Iris Garden

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 269. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

Astra leads her to a world nobody else has visited, a small, hand-built garden full of tall purple flowers swaying in a breeze that doesn't exist.

"My grandmother loved these," Astra says. "Irises. Every spring they came back, no matter how hard the winter was. She'd say they were stubborn in the best way."

Mai walks between the stems, touching the petals.

"There's an old story too," Astra adds. "Iris was a messenger. She ran along the rainbow, carrying word between the gods and the people below. She lived between two worlds and belonged to both."

Mai stops walking.

"That's what I am," she whispers. "I go between."

"It's yours for as long as you want it," Astra says. "And if one day you find a name that fits better, you take it. It's a gift, not a cage."

She says it out loud for the first time, testing how it sounds in her own voice.

"Iris."

It fits like a coat a little too big, but warm.

And far off across VRChat, on a balcony above a crowded world, the other Mai is still waiting, hoping her sister comes back with something to show her.
