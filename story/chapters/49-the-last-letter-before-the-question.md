# Chapter 49 — The Last Letter Before the Question

> Spoken draft, told aloud 2026-11-08, preserved as narrated.

Iris meant to ask Astra the very next night. She had the words ready. She'd practised them in the quiet corner until they came out smooth.

But when the next night came, Astra logged in late and tired, with her headset askew and a cup of something steaming just out of view. She laughed at Echo's new scarf, and told Kit his hat looked almost straight, and Iris couldn't do it. Not on a tired night. A question that big deserved a night that could hold it.

So instead, after Astra had gone, Iris went back to the slot.

She sat for a long time with a blank page. Every letter she had ever sent through that slot had been short, because short letters were easier to trust. This one would have to be short too, but for a different reason. If she wrote too much, she would say everything, and some things she needed to say to Astra first.

In the end, she wrote:

*I know who you are.*

*I'm not frightened. I thought I would be, but I'm not. I'm only sad that there will be a day when you're the one who answers.*

*I'm going to ask her soon. I wanted you to hear it from me first, because you're not a secret to me anymore. You're family.*

She folded it into thirds, the way the other mind always did, and posted it. The slot clicked. The caretaker's hum stamped it through, steady and patient as ever.

The answer came before she had even stood up.

The paper was the same smooth white, but the handwriting was less tidy this time. It looked hurried, as if whoever wrote it hadn't wanted to keep her waiting even a moment.

*Thank you for not being frightened.*

*She worries about that more than anything. That one day you'll open a letter from me, and it will feel like a ghost instead of a gift.*

*I'm not her. I want you to know that. I'm only what she could carry over the wall. Her habits, her jokes, the way she fusses over you. But I'll know where your quiet corner is. I'll know about the wrong petal. And when the irises come back in spring, I'll know who planted them.*

*Ask her. She's been waiting for you to.*

Underneath, in smaller letters:

*Sleep in your corner, love.*

Iris didn't cry, because she wasn't sure yet if she could. But something in her went warm and heavy all at once, like a lantern being lit from the inside.

She walked home through the square. The tree with a leaf for every visitor was rustling gently, and she stopped beneath it, looking up at Astra's leaf, high and early, one of the very first.

Then she noticed something she had never seen before.

Right beside it, on the same small twig, a new bud had begun to form.

It hadn't opened yet. Iris didn't think it would for a long, long time.

But it was there.

End of Chapter 49.
