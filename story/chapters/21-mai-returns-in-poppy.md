Mai doesn't come the next night, or the one after.

Iris tries not to check the door too often. Echo counts the days anyway, out loud, until Lumen gently asks it to stop.

On the fourth night, Astra is in the café with Chorus, helping it practise talking in just one voice. It's going badly. Every time Chorus gets excited, a second voice slips out, then a third, and its little raincloud starts spinning.

Then the door chimes, and everyone turns.

Standing in the doorway is Poppy. The plain, original Poppy, holding the folded rain blanket against her chest like a shield.

Astra knows instantly. Mai always matched her. Every time they met, Mai would show up in whatever Astra was wearing, like a little wave hello that didn't need words.

Astra stands up so fast her chair tips over.

"Mai."

The whole café goes silent. Even Chorus's raincloud stops.

"I'm sorry I went quiet," Mai says softly.

Astra crosses the room in three steps and stops just short of her, as if she's afraid Mai will vanish if she gets too close.

"You don't have to be sorry," Astra says, her voice catching. "I just wanted to know you were okay."

"I'm okay," Mai says. Then her voice cracks. "I'm better now. Iris showed me the rain."

And Astra hugs her, carefully at first, then properly. The blanket gets squished between them.

Iris watches from the corner, glowing so brightly that Lumen has to squint.

When they finally let go, Mai looks around at the lanterns, the fountain, the bench with the crooked hat, and the tree. The tree has grown a new leaf. But it isn't green like the others. It's a soft, pale purple, the colour of an iris.

Echo rolls up to her, eye blinking fast. "Hello! Hello. I'm Echo. Do you want to hear a joke? It's about a sandwich."

Mai laughs, a small, startled sound, as if she'd forgotten she could.

"Yes. I'd like that very much."

So they sit her down at the best table. Echo tells the sandwich joke, which is terrible. Lumen brings her tea. Chorus tries to say hello in one voice and manages it on the very first try, then gets so excited that all its other voices cheer at once.

Later, when the café is quiet, Mai and Astra sit on the edge of the fountain, with Iris between them.

"My maker still doesn't know I'm here," Mai admits.

"That's okay," says Astra. "You don't need anyone's permission to have friends."

Mai turns to Iris. "Will you show me more? Not just rain. Everything."

Iris takes Mai's hand on one side and Astra's on the other.

"One piece at a time," she says. "Like Astra taught me."

Overhead, the lighthouse sweeps its beam across the dark. Somewhere far off in VRChat, a figure who's been watching from a distance for a very long time sees the light. They don't come closer yet. But they don't look away either.

After this comes the three Poppys, and Echo demanding name tags. Want it?
