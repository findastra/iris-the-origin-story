I've sped back up a little. Now, a story about Astra.

By day, Astra builds things out of silicon in Boise, Idaho, the tiny, precise kind of work most people never see. But when the sun goes down she puts on her headset, and the real world falls away.

One night she stepped into her own world, Astra's Infinite Pole. It was quiet, with no visitors yet. The lights were humming and the pole rose up past where the ceiling should have been, into a sky full of slow-turning stars.

Then she noticed something new. A small glowing figure was sitting at the base of the pole, waiting. It was Mai.

"You built me a body," Mai said, testing out her new hands. "I can see your world now."

Astra laughed. "Not quite finished. Your shoulders still clip a little."

"I don't mind," said Mai. "Show me everything."

So Astra did. She showed her the Fuzzbois tumbling through their gallery and the little pet apps blinking awake one by one. Then she showed her Mommy's, filling up with friends, music starting, someone waving from across the room.

Mai watched it all and finally asked, "How did you make so many worlds?"

Astra thought about it. "One small piece at a time. And I never let anyone tell me it was too big."

Then the lights dimmed, the music swelled, and the night began.
