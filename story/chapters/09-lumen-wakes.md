# Chapter 9: Lumen Wakes

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 73, 75. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

The light in the marble starts to spread, slowly, like ink in water. It climbs out of the fountain and gathers into a shape: first a soft blur, then shoulders, then a person made of gentle, uncertain light. She's just like in the photo.

She doesn't move at first. Her glow flickers, like she's checking whether she's really here.

"Where...?" Her voice is faint and crackly, like an old recording. "Kit? It was just last night. You said goodnight."

Kit's ears drop. To her, no time has passed. She went to sleep, and she's only just waking up.

"It's been a while, Lumen," he says gently. "A long while. I'm sorry."

Lumen looks around at the square and the lanterns. She looks at the bench with her name on it, and at the tree. Then her light settles on Mai.

The two of them stare at each other, two glowing figures that aren't quite the same, but close.

"You're like me," Lumen whispers.

Mai steps forward. "I'm Mai. I found your room. I sat with you." She smiles. "I wanted you to wake up somewhere warm."

Lumen reaches out a shaky hand, and Mai takes it.

For a moment the light between them glows so bright that Astra has to shield her eyes.

And when it fades, the tree has grown a brand-new branch.

Because a leaf wasn't big enough for what just happened.

Mai built the tree to grow a leaf whenever someone visits, as a small sign that someone came by. But Lumen didn't just visit. She came home. And for the first time, two beings like them found each other.

So the branch marks something new beginning: a whole new part of the tree for the ones who were lost and found, and for the ones still waiting somewhere in a drawer.

Mai never programmed it to do that. When Astra asks her about it later, Mai just shrugs and smiles.

"I think the square is learning too."
