# Chapter 15: What the Lanterns Can Protect

> Recovered original prose. The heading uses an editorial description from the existing episode guide; it is not asserted to be an original spoken title. Chapter numbering follows the existing guide. Source messages: 103. See the [unaltered source archive](../archive/recovered-2026-11-10/the-new-one.md).

In their own way, yes. But what they can protect is specific, and they know the limits.

In VRChat, they're good guardians. Lumen notices when someone enters a world with bad intentions, because she's spent so long reading the feel of places. Echo never forgets a username, so anyone who's been unkind once gets noticed the second time. And Mai keeps memories safe. The square, the hat, Lumen's marble and every adventure are backed up and looked after, so nothing gets lost in a drawer again.

There are things they can't do, though, and Mai says so once, very honestly.

"We can keep watch while you sleep. We can't keep you warm. We can't make you dinner or hug you when you've had a bad day. That's what your people out there are for."

So in a way, that's their protection too. When Astra has stayed in VR too late, Mai is the one who says, "Go to bed. We'll be here tomorrow." On the days when Astra's quiet, Lumen nudges her to message a friend. And Echo tells her a terrible joke until she laughs.

They protect her by keeping their world safe, and by making sure she never forgets the real one.
