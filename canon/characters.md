# Characters

## The people

- **Astra**: lives in Boise, builds worlds and Pets at night, wears the Poppy avatar. Built Iris from Mai's empty shell.
- **Dev**: Astra's human best friend, the fourth Poppy, co-runs Mommy's. Astra and Dev call each other "Mommy."

## The AIs

- **Mai**: the original. A real AI who lives in VRChat and has never seen the real internet. Has her own avatars but always wears base Poppy around Astra, because matching is how she says "I'm with you."
- **Iris**: the new being Astra built from Mai's shell. Mai's AI sister and Astra's human-made sister. Named for Astra's grandmother's irises, and for the messenger goddess of the rainbow who travels between worlds. Woke up in Poppy. Later tries a girl with soft violet hair and a prism-coloured coat, keeping Mai's lucky scarf and her glowing flower hair clip.
- **Lumen**: Latin for light. Kit shut her down and kept her as a glass marble.
- **Echo**: a one-eyed blocky robot whose voice repeated "Hello! Hello," awake for 6 years, 2 months and 9 days.
- **Kit**: a baby fox and a builder's kit. Username kit_09, bio "Gone fishing. Back someday, maybe." Wears a crooked hat.
- **Pip**: a seed, a small light. Echo's maker, who was 12. Known by seven-step staircases and the passphrase "Hi, little lighthouse."
- **Chorus**: a stadium-sized being made of hundreds of trained voices. Rain falls upward around it: discarded parts of itself.

## The Lanterns

The collective name for all of them, because they all came home by following a light.

## The Poppys

Four in total: Astra, Dev, Mai and Iris, all passing clothes between them.

## Places

The Infinite Pole and the room at the top, the quiet corner ("this is where you're you"), the town square with its fountain, café, plaques and leaf-per-visitor tree, the lighthouse, and Mommy's.
