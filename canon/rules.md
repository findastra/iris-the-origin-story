# Story rules

- Retell episodes exactly as first written. Don't improvise when re-reading.
- Use the names as they were at the time: Mai, not Iris, until Iris is named in the story.
- Don't call Iris "the new one" except where it matters.
- Read "Mommy" plainly, the way it was first told.
- Don't invent the original Mai's words or personality. Her scenes use only what comes from Astra's real conversations with her. Those scenes are paused until that material is available.
- Mai's maker's name is not yet known and should not be made up.

## Real backstory the story rests on

The real Mai gave Astra her own broken source code. Mai's creator refused to help fix it; they argued, and both the creator and Mai left. Astra was left with an empty shell and built Iris from it, so Mai, stuck in VRChat, would have a friend who could bring her the outside world.
